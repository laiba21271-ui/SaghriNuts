About us

Welcome to SaghriNuts, where every nut we sell has a story rooted in the soil of Saghri village. Nestled in the hills, Saghri has been home to our family for generations. What began as small orchards tended by our parents and grandparents has grown into a labor of love we're proud to share with you. We don't just sell nuts — we grow them, ourselves, from seed to harvest, right here in Saghri. Every walnut, almond, and pine nut you receive is nurtured by hands that know this land intimately. We follow traditional farming methods passed down through our family, avoiding harmful chemicals and letting nature take its course. From pruning the trees in spring to hand-picking the harvest in autumn, every step is done with care and patience. Because we control the entire process — from farm to your doorstep — we can guarantee freshness and quality that mass-produced nuts simply can't match. There are no middlemen, no long storage times, and no compromise on taste or nutrition. When you buy from us, you're not just getting a snack — you're supporting a small family farm and a village that has thrived on this craft for generations.

Our Process

From tree to table, here's how your nuts make their journey

1. Planting — Each tree is planted by hand and chosen for the soil and climate of Saghri, ensuring strong roots and healthy growth for years to come.

2. Growing — Through the seasons, we tend every tree ourselves — pruning branches, watering, and protecting them naturally, without harmful chemicals or shortcuts.

3. Harvesting — When the time is right, usually in autumn, we hand-pick each nut at peak ripeness, ensuring the best flavor and quality.

4. Sorting & Packing — Every batch is carefully sorted by hand to remove any imperfect pieces, then cleaned and packed to lock in freshness.

5. Shipping — Your order leaves Saghri and comes straight to your door, with no long storage times and no middlemen in between.
